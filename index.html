TRANSPARENCY & CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, honest answers about my background, work philosophy, and availability.

01

Why did you combine Software Engineering with an Accounting & Finance degree?

Software is fundamentally about business logic and asset movement. Having formal training in accounting (Comptabilité, Finance & Fiscalité - DEUG FEG) enables me to architect FinTech applications, ERPs, and automated payment pipelines with a deep understanding of Moroccan taxation (TVA, IS, IR), debit/credit mechanisms, and balance-sheet diagnostics without needing an analyst to translate business requirements.

02

What is unique about Awal GPT for Moroccan Darija and Amazigh?

Standard commercial LLMs perform poorly on Moroccan Darija and Amazigh due to dialectal code-switching and sparse web corpora. Awal GPT integrates an official IRCAM lexical database of 9,000+ entries, optimized with RapidFuzz semantic proximity, spaCy linguistic filtering, and local RAG Qwen2.5 execution on CPU, providing authentic, culturally aware answers.

03

How does your hands-on GPON fiber experience help with full-stack development?

Field experience with physical telecom networks (GPON optical splitters, OLT/ONT configuration, OTDR reflectometry, VLAN segmentation) demystifies cloud infrastructure. It allows me to design distributed APIs with low latency, anticipate network bottlenecks, implement strict SSL/TLS encryption, and architect resilient architectures.

04

Are you available for internships, full-time roles, or freelance contracts?

Yes! Currently completing my Bachelor GIGD at EST Kénitra, I am actively available for high-impact software engineering roles, FinTech internships, startup collaborations, and freelance missions in Kénitra, Rabat, Casablanca, or remotely worldwide.

05

What is your primary technology stack for client deployments?

My core stack covers Python (Django, FastAPI, scikit-learn, spaCy), TypeScript/JavaScript (React, Next.js, Node.js), Mobile (React Native, Expo, Flutter), Databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite), alongside Docker, Git CI/CD, and Linux deployment environments.

06

What languages do you speak and how do you collaborate in international teams?

I am native bilingual in Arabic and Tamazight, fully proficient in professional French (B2/C1), and fluent in technical English. Through Enactus and international freelance projects, I work smoothly across multicultural teams and agile Scrum ceremonies.
